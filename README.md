Epistatic Fitness Landscapes and Isoeliteness-guided Genomic Selection (isoGS) <br />

Author: Ted Monyak<br />
Crop Adaptation Lab<br />
Colorado State University<br />
Soil and Crop Sciences Department<br />
Advisor: Dr. Geoff Morris<br />

This work contains breeding simulations
that investigate how the dynamics of adaptive walks affect the trait architecture
of complex traits in breeding metapopulations.<br />


scripts/SimulationPipeline.R is the simulation pipeline associated with "Emergence of epistatic fitness landscapes from parallel adaptive walks in breeding network metapopulations".


scripts/GwpPipeline.R is the simulation pipeline associated with "isoGS" (recently submitted).
